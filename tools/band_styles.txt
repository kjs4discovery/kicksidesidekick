# The band's tier tables for backtracks (M9, D052): per style, per tier 1..4, what harmony, bass and drums play in one
# bar. The user edits this file by ear; tools/band_styles.py reads it and generates firmware/app/band_styles.h (C) and
# tools/fm1song/band_styles_data.py (Python), so the FM-1 and the laptop cannot drift. See docs/plan-M9.md, Part 3.
#
# STYLE <name> steps <per beat> [swing]     steps 4: sixteenths (16 characters a 4/4 bar, 12 a 3/4 bar); steps 3:
#                                           triplet eighths (12 characters a 4/4 bar). WALTZ is 3/4; the others 4/4.
# TIER 1..4, each with a line per layer: HARM, BASS and the drum rows K S H O R C Y T (kick 36, snare 38, closed hat 42,
# open hat 46, rim 37, clap 39, ride 51, tom lo 45). A row not listed is silent.
# A line holds one bar, or two bars separated by " | " (odd bars the first, even the second, counting from the start of
# each section).
# HARM <CLOSE|WIDE|UPPER> then steps: x the chord (vel 72), o (58), g ghost (44); l / m / h the lowest / middle /
#   highest note of the WIDE voicing (58); - the previous hit goes on sounding; . silence. A hit lasts its step and the
#   '-' after it; a note is cut at a chord change.
# BASS steps: R root, F fifth, O octave, T third, N the next chord's root, A a semitone below the next chord's root;
#   upper case vel 88, lower case 68; - and . as harmony.
# Drum rows: x (92), o (70), g (34), . silence; a drum note is one step long.
#
