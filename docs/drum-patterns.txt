# Our own patterns, written from common genre conventions; the user listens and prunes (docs/controls.md lists them).
#
#   pattern NAME BEATS STEPS      BEATS: beats a bar (the tempo counts them); STEPS: steps a bar (16, or 12 for
#                                 triplet feels: 3 a beat in 4/4, or 6/8's 2 x 6)
#   KICK  x...o...|x...o...       one row per instrument, any order, missing rows are silent; a second bar after "|"
#                                 (1 or 2 bars); spaces are ignored
#   x accent   o normal   g ghost (soft)   . rest
# Instruments: KICK SNARE CHH (closed hat) OHH (open hat) CLAP TOMLO TOMHI RIM (rim / percussion)
