# Code of conduct

KickSide is a small project for people who make music. We want everyone to feel welcome in its issues, discussions and
pull requests, whatever their age, experience, background, ability or identity.

We follow the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1** (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The standard it sets is summed up here:

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners. Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Assume good faith, and disagree about ideas without attacking people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, sexualised language or attention, personal attacks, or publishing someone's
  private information.
- Keep to the topic. No advertising, and no spam.

**Scope.** This applies to every space of the project (the repository, its issues, discussions and pull requests, and
the website) and to anyone representing the project in public.

**Enforcement.** The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may temporarily or
permanently block anyone who breaks these rules. To report a problem, write privately to
websiteInfo@KickSideSideKick.com. Reports are read in confidence and handled fairly, and the reporter's privacy is
respected.
